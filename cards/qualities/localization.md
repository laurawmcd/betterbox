# Localization

*This card is proposed for the next version of the deck.*

Localization asks whether the system supports multiple languages or locally appropriate formats throughout. That covers scripts and text direction, dates, numbers, currency and units, and the wording of messages. Translation needs people who know the context, and someone to check it.

## Questions to ask

- Which languages and formats must the system support?
- Does it handle the scripts and text directions we need?
- Who translates, and who checks?
- Are dates, numbers, currency and units shown in the way people here expect?

## In a workshop

Ask a speaker of each target language to use the system and mark everything that looks or reads wrongly.
