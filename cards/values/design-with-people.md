# Design with people

Good design involves the different groups who will manage, use and benefit from the project, in the first design and in every iteration. That means creating ways for people to improve what has been built, keeping feedback and grievance channels that are actually monitored and answered, and working in short cycles that respond to what people say. Respect, agency and autonomy are part of it: people should be treated as participants with their own decisions to make.

## Questions to ask

- Which groups need to take part, and have we asked them?
- How can people give feedback or complain, and who reads it?
- What did we change last time because of what people told us?
- Where are we making decisions on people's behalf?

This card matches one of the nine [Principles for Digital Development](https://digitalprinciples.org/principles/design-with-people/).
