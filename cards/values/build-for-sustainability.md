# Build for sustainability

*This card is proposed for the next version of the deck.*

Build for the long term by planning for financial, operational and ecological sustainability from the start. Show the full cost of ownership openly, plan for scale, consider how the project adapts to climate change and reduces its footprint, and accept that sustainable sometimes means consolidating services, transferring knowledge or planning a transition rather than running forever.

## Questions to ask

- What does this cost to run each year, and who pays?
- What happens when the funding ends?
- Who will hold the knowledge when the team changes?
- Is there a planned end or handover?

This card matches one of the nine [Principles for Digital Development](https://digitalprinciples.org/principles/build-for-sustainability/).
