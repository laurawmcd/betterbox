# Understand the existing ecosystem

*This card is proposed for the next version of the deck.*

Trust starts with a thorough understanding of the setting you work in: culture, gender norms, politics, the economy, technology infrastructure, and who already does what. Review government policies and sector strategies, support the local organizations and technology already tackling the problem, assess device access, connectivity, affordability and digital literacy, and keep reassessing, because ecosystems change. Skipping this step leads to exclusion, lost trust and risk for the people involved.

## Questions to ask

- Who is already working on this problem, and what do they use?
- Which government policies and strategies apply?
- What do people have access to, and what can they afford?
- When did we last check that our picture is still true?

This card matches one of the nine [Principles for Digital Development](https://digitalprinciples.org/principles/understand-the-existing-ecosystem/).
