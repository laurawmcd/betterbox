# Design for inclusion

Inclusive design accounts for disability, literacy, language, connectivity, income and culture. It also reaches the offline people who benefit or lose indirectly, and it treats capacity-building and the cost of devices and services as part of the design. Diversity, gender, disability and intersectionality all belong under this value.

## Questions to ask

- Who is left out by the way this is designed?
- How do disability, literacy, language and cost change who can use it?
- Who benefits or loses without being online at all?
- What help and training do people need to take part?

This card matches one of the nine [Principles for Digital Development](https://digitalprinciples.org/principles/design-for-inclusion/).
