# Leave no one behind

*This card is proposed for the next version of the deck.*

Projects that succeed on average often fail the people with the least access, income, connectivity or voice. Leaving no one behind means asking who would be missing if the project met its own targets, and giving them priority in design and in measurement.

## Questions to ask

- Who is hardest to reach, and why?
- Which groups would be missing if we hit our targets?
- Are they counted, and can we see their results separately?
- What would we change to reach them first?
