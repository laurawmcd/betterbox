# Create open and transparent practices

Trust and good governance come from defining roles and responsibilities with accountability, sharing decisions, policies and practices openly, and giving stakeholders ways to ask questions and raise concerns. Technical practice follows: agile methods, open standards, open data, open source and open innovation where they fit. Where a project is closed, people stop trusting it and stop taking part.

## Questions to ask

- What do we share publicly about decisions, funding and performance?
- Who can ask a question, and who answers?
- Which parts could be open source, open data or built on open standards?
- What are we keeping private, and is there a good reason?

This card matches one of the nine [Principles for Digital Development](https://digitalprinciples.org/principles/create-open-and-transparent-practices/).
