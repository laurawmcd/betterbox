# Anticipate and mitigate harms

Harm is always possible with technology, and technology never operates alone. This value asks the team to look for harms before launch: digital repression and surveillance, widening divides by disability, income or geography, technology-enabled gender-based violence, damage to local businesses and civil society, and amplified social norms. Mitigation needs technical, regulatory, policy and institutional safeguards, and a long view of how today's inequities could grow.

## Questions to ask

- What is the worst plausible use of this system, by us or by others?
- Whom could it harm first, and how would we hear about it?
- Which safeguards are technical, which legal, and which institutional?
- How might this look in five years, with more users and more data?

This card matches one of the nine [Principles for Digital Development](https://digitalprinciples.org/principles/anticipate-and-mitigate-harms/).
