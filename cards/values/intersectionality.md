# Intersectionality

*This card is proposed for the next version of the deck.*

People stand at the crossing of gender, disability, age, class, ethnicity and more, and the overlaps can compound disadvantage in ways that single categories miss. A project that serves 'women' or 'the poor' in general may still fail a poor disabled woman. This value asks the team to look at combinations, not only at categories.

## Questions to ask

- Which combinations of identity or circumstance change how someone experiences this?
- Where do our categories hide differences?
- Whose experience do our data not show?
- Who could tell us what we cannot see?
