# Local ownership

*This card is proposed for the next version of the deck.*

Localization here means more than translation. It asks whether local people and organizations have a hand in leading the work, who owns what is built, and whether the design fits local knowledge, language and priorities. Projects run from elsewhere can end when the outside team leaves.

## Questions to ask

- Who locally leads this, and who owns it?
- Who benefits, and who profits?
- Does the design fit local knowledge and priorities?
- What stays if the outside team leaves?
