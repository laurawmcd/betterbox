# Use evidence to improve outcomes

Evidence drives impact. Monitoring and evaluation now look at outcomes for people and communities, not only at access and usage. Use several ways of gathering feedback, both digital and analogue, keep complaint channels that someone watches, involve people in designing how success is measured, and change the project when the evidence says so.

## Questions to ask

- What outcomes for people are we measuring, and what only counts usage?
- How do we collect feedback from people who are not online?
- Who helped decide what to measure?
- What would make us change course?

This card matches one of the nine [Principles for Digital Development](https://digitalprinciples.org/principles/use-evidence-to-improve-outcomes/).
