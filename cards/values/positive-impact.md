# Aim for positive impact

Avoiding harm is the minimum. This card asks for more: do better than no harm, and make the project better than not using technology at all. Beneficence is the old word for it. The test is whether the people affected would say that they are better off.

## Questions to ask

- What would be better for people in a year because this project exists?
- Compared with doing nothing, or with a non-technical alternative, is this better?
- Who would say it made things worse?
- How will we know?
