# Agency and autonomy

*This card is proposed for the next version of the deck.*

Agency is a person's ability to act on their own behalf. Autonomy is their freedom to decide. Technology can support both, for example by giving people control of their data and real choices about whether and how to use a service, or it can undermine them through nudging, lock-in and hidden defaults.

## Questions to ask

- What choices do people have, and are they real?
- Is anyone being nudged, pressured or locked in?
- Can people leave, and take their data with them?
- Who decides the defaults?
