# Respect

*This card is proposed for the next version of the deck.*

Respect shows up in small design decisions: whether people were asked, whether consent is real, whether their time and knowledge are valued, and whether the system treats them with dignity. It is easiest to see in how people are addressed and heard at their worst moments.

## Questions to ask

- Did the people affected have a say in this?
- Is their consent real, or a click-through?
- Is their time, knowledge and effort acknowledged or paid for?
- Does the language in the interface treat them with dignity?
