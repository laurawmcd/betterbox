# Accountability and governance

Accountability means someone owns each decision and answers for it. Governance is the set of structures that make that real: who decides, who oversees, and how the people affected can raise concerns and get a response. Without both, good intentions have nothing to hold on to.

## Questions to ask

- Who is responsible for each major decision?
- To whom do they answer, and how?
- How can an affected person raise a concern, and what happens next?
- Who steps in if the people responsible fail?
