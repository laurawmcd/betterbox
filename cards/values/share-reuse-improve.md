# Share, reuse and improve

Before building something new, look for something that already works. Use open standards and open source software, contribute improvements back, and document your work in a way others can follow. Reuse saves time and money, encourages collaboration and produces better products through continuous improvement.

## Questions to ask

- What already exists that we could use or adapt?
- Who could reuse what we make, and is it documented for them?
- Which open standards or open source tools apply?
- What will we contribute back?

This card matches one of the nine [Principles for Digital Development](https://digitalprinciples.org/principles/share-reuse-and-improve/).
