# Climate and environment

Technology has an environmental footprint in the devices it needs, the energy it uses, the servers it runs on and the waste it leaves. Projects also work in places affected by climate change. This value asks the team to reduce the footprint where they can, and to plan for the weather and hazards the project will face.

## Questions to ask

- What devices, energy and hosting does the project depend on?
- What happens to the equipment at the end of its life?
- Which climate risks affect the users and the infrastructure?
- Could a lower-tech option do the same job?
