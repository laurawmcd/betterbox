# Be collaborative

*This card is proposed for the next version of the deck.*

Many projects duplicate work others have already done or are doing. Being collaborative means sharing information, insight and resources with others working on related problems, and drawing on expertise from other organizations and sectors. It saves effort and spares users from a scatter of unconnected services.

## Questions to ask

- Who else is working on this problem, and have we talked to them?
- What could we share, and what could we borrow?
- Who has the expertise we lack?
- What would it take to combine efforts?
