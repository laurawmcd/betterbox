# Connectivity

Signal, bandwidth and power decide what can work. Find out which networks reach your users, how reliable and how expensive they are, and whether people rely on basic phones, shared devices or public access points. Design for the connection people actually have rather than the one you would like them to have.

## Questions to ask

- What coverage, speed and cost do users experience in practice?
- Do they use basic phones or shared devices?
- What should keep working offline or over SMS?
- What happens to the project when the connection drops?

## In a workshop

Test a key task on a slow or intermittent connection before the workshop, and bring the results.
