# Establish people-first data practices

Digital services and initiatives generate, rely on and use people's data. It is critical to put people's rights and needs first when collecting, sharing, analyzing or deleting it, and to follow the applicable rules and guidelines. People-first data practices include meaningful consent and real control over how data is used, managed and maintained.

This card is also one of the nine Principles for Digital Development.

## Questions to ask

- What data do we need, and what could we choose not to collect?
- How do people give meaningful consent, and can they withdraw it?
- Can people see, correct and take their data elsewhere?
- Which data protection rules apply, and who checks that we follow them?

## In a workshop

Choose one data field, such as a phone number, and ask the group to justify collecting it.
