# Cultural context

Culture determines who is allowed to own a phone, whom people trust, what is private, and how information travels. What looks like a technical problem may turn out to be a cultural one, and design choices that ignore local norms can exclude people or put them at risk.

## Questions to ask

- Whose permission or endorsement matters in this community?
- How do gender norms affect who can own devices, use the service or speak up?
- What counts as private or sensitive here?
- Could any words, symbols or colours in the design mean something different locally?

## In a workshop

Ask someone from the community, or who has lived there, to react to the system map and point out what is missing.
