# Legal issues

Ask what local, regional, national or international law applies. Some sectors have their own rules, for example for health information. Look at legislation in the places you work, and find out whether laws in the jurisdictions where data is stored or passes through, such as a cloud provider's region, also apply.

## Questions to ask

- Which laws apply where we work, and where our data is held or sent?
- Do any domain-specific rules apply, such as health, finance or children's data?
- Who on the team can read the relevant law, or who could we ask?
- What changes if we move the data to another country or provider?

## In a workshop

List every place the data lives or travels, and mark each with the law that applies. Blank spaces are homework.
