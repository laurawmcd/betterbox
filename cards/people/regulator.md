# Regulator

Regulators include data protection authorities, telecoms regulators, health and financial authorities and local government. Their rules can require things you have not planned for, and in some places they can stop a project. Regulators may also be part of the political context, so look at how they act as well as what the rules say.

## Questions to ask

- Which regulators have a say over this project, in which places?
- What do they require before launch and while it runs?
- Have you spoken to them, or to someone who has?
- What happens to the project if the rules change?

## In a workshop

List the regulators on a flipchart and mark the ones nobody in the room has spoken to.
