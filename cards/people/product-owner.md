# Product owner / Product manager

The product owner or product manager decides what the project builds first, what waits, and what gets dropped. They manage deadlines and daily decisions, organize ideas and plans, and gather feedback and input from everyone else on the list.

Often the role is held part-time by someone with another job, or split between a funder's expectations and a team's capacity. Working out who holds it, and how much authority they have, tells you where decisions are really made.

## Questions to ask

- Who makes the final call on priorities, and do they know it?
- How does this person hear from the people who will use the system?
- What happens when the funder's requirements and the users' needs pull in different directions?
- Does this person have the time and authority the role needs?

## In a workshop

Put this card on the table early and ask the group to name the person, or to admit that nobody holds the role. Come back to it when you reach prioritization.
