# Governance and oversight

*This card is proposed for the next version of the deck.*

Boards, steering groups, ethics committees, community panels and donor oversight all review a project and can act when it goes wrong. What matters is whether they receive real information, hold real authority, and are independent of the people running the project.

## Questions to ask

- Who oversees this project, and who oversees them?
- What information do they receive, and how often?
- Can the people affected bring concerns to them directly?
- What powers do they have when something goes wrong?

## In a workshop

Draw the lines of accountability on the system map and mark where no line exists.
