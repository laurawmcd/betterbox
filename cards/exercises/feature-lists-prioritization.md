# Feature lists and prioritization

Using your personas and system map, list what the project may need to do. Writing each feature as a user story ('As a [user], I need to [do something in the system] so that I can [purpose]') helps developers understand why they are being asked to build it.

**Time:** 30 minutes to list, 15 minutes to prioritize

## How to run it

1. Give each person sticky notes in one colour. Ask them to write features for the personas, drawing on the system map and their own ideas, and attach them to the persona they belong to. Share and discuss.
2. To prioritize, use one of two methods. In the first, give each participant a fixed number of stickers (10 or fewer). Without discussing, they vote on the notes they think matter, using all their stickers and stacking more than one on the same note if they wish. If you have no stickers, people can draw stars in marker pen.
3. In the second method, take each note and ask the group to place it higher or lower on a flipchart, where higher means more important. Do this together with discussion, or silently, which encourages everyone to take part. In the silent version people may move notes placed by others. If a note keeps moving up and down, put a mark on it and discuss it in plenary.
4. In plenary, ask what was prioritized, what was deprioritized, and what surprised people. Ask a few people why they voted as they did.
5. End with a prioritized display that you can hand to the developers or use to shape your feedback to them.

## Tips

- The deck card says to sort features as high, medium or low. Either voting method gives you that.
