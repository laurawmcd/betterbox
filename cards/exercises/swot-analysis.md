# SWOT analysis

*This card is proposed for the next version of the deck.*

A SWOT analysis sorts what you know about the project into four boxes. Strengths and weaknesses are inside your control. Opportunities and threats come from outside.

## How to run it

1. Draw a grid with four boxes: Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities and Threats.
2. In small groups, fill each box, using sticky notes.
3. Compare the grids. Look for weaknesses that a threat could exploit and strengths that could open an opportunity.
4. Pick the most important items and decide what to do about them.

## Tips

- Use Threat modeling to go deeper on the Threats box.
