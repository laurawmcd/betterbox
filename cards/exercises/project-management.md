# Project management

Project management is less an exercise than a practice. Setting clear goals, planning and monitoring progress, naming owners and managing stakeholders, and identifying and mitigating risk are the habits that make technology projects better.

## How to run it

1. Go through each practice: clear goals and underlying objectives, planning and monitoring progress, clear ownership and stakeholder management, and identifying and mitigating risks.
2. For each, ask the group how well the project does it today.
3. Discuss whether strengthening any of them in the management of the project might lead to better outcomes, and agree who will act.
