# Plenary discussion

*This card is proposed for the next version of the deck.*

After the exercises, return to the whole group. Go back to the system map and to whatever you are testing, such as a demo or prototype, and ask what feedback the feature list did not capture.

**Time:** 45 minutes

## How to run it

1. Return to the system map you drew of the new reality, and to the demo or plan under discussion. Ask what feedback people have that the feature list does not cover.
2. Ask about operational and political challenges. Tease out concerns about surveillance in people's jobs, about security and the protection of people, and about trust in the state and in others.
3. Be open to any concern and any criticism.
4. If the discussion turns negative, be ready to answer objections. You can end on the voting results from the prioritization, which give solid feedback.

## Tips

- Ending on a negative note is a real risk. Plan the end of the session so that people leave with a clear list of what they will do.
