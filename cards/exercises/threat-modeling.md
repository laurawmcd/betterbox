# Threat modeling

Threat modeling looks at the project from the point of view of someone who wants to misuse it. Mapping those actors helps you find and reduce risks that cheerful planning tends to miss.

## How to run it

1. Identify the assets in the project: data, systems, money, reputations, people.
2. Identify the vulnerabilities that might expose those assets to risk.
3. Identify the bad actors who might exploit the vulnerabilities, and what each of them wants.
4. For each combination that worries the group, agree what to do, and add it to your risk list.
5. Turn it on its head: identify the actors who could gain value from the project succeeding, and think about how to work with them.
